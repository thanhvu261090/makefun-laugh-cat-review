A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990) bounty entry: one funny single-line one-star review written in the voice of a house cat about its human owner, under 200 characters.

## The entry

> One star. He filled my bowl, then ate his own dinner right in front of me like the food was ever his. I knocked his wine off the table. Fair trade.

147 characters, one line.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) validates the entry against the bounty gate — 1–200 characters, a single line, not only whitespace, valid UTF-8 — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
